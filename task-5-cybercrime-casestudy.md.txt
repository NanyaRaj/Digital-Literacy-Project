Phishing is a common type of cybercrime in which attackers trick individuals into sharing sensitive information such as passwords, bank details, or OTPs. It usually takes place through fake emails, messages, or websites that appear to be from trusted organizations. A common example involves a college student receiving an email that looks like it is from their bank. The email states that their account will be blocked unless they verify their details immediately. It also provides a link that redirects the user to a fake website designed to look exactly like the official bank page. Without realizing the risk, the student enters their login credentials and OTP. Once the information is submitted, it is directly captured by the attacker. Within a short time, unauthorized transactions may take place, leading to financial loss. In some cases, personal data may also be misused for identity theft. Students and young internet users are often targeted because they may not always verify the authenticity of such messages. The main reasons behind these attacks are lack of awareness and urgency created by the scammer. This case highlights the importance of being cautious while using digital platforms. Users should always verify the source of emails, avoid clicking on suspicious links, and never share confidential information online. Awareness and careful behavior can significantly reduce the chances of falling victim to phishing attacks.
